About the Plastering Calculator

The Toolaxa Plastering Calculator helps estimate cement, sand and labour requirements for internal wall plaster, external wall plaster and ceiling plaster. It also calculates approximate material cost and total labour cost using your entered rates.

How to Use This Calculator

  • Enter internal wall length, height and number of plastered sides.
  • Enter total door and window opening area for internal walls.
  • Enter external wall dimensions and plastered sides.
  • Enter ceiling length and width.
  • Select wall and ceiling plaster thickness.
  • Choose the cement-sand mortar ratio.
  • Enter cement and sand market rates.
  • Enter separate wall and ceiling labour rates per sq.ft.
  • Click Calculate Total Cost to see your complete estimate.
  • Download the calculation as CSV or print/save it as PDF.

Calculation Method

Wall plaster area is calculated using wall length × wall height × number of plastered sides, minus the specified opening area. Ceiling area is calculated using ceiling length × ceiling width.

Wet mortar volume is calculated from plaster area and plaster thickness. A dry volume factor is applied to estimate dry mortar. Cement and sand quantities are calculated using the selected mortar ratio and the entered wastage percentage.

Wall labour cost is calculated using the combined internal and external plaster area multiplied by the wall labour rate. Ceiling labour cost is calculated using the ceiling area multiplied by the ceiling labour rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does this calculator include labour charges?

Yes. It calculates wall and ceiling plaster labour separately using your entered rate per square foot.

2. Can I enter different labour rates for wall and ceiling plaster?

Yes. Separate labour rates can be entered for wall plaster and ceiling plaster.

3. Which plaster thickness should I select?

Common wall plaster thicknesses include 12 mm and 15 mm for internal work, while external plaster may use 15 mm or more according to project specifications. Ceiling thickness should also follow the site specification.

4. Which cement-sand ratio is supported?

The calculator supports 1:3, 1:4, 1:5 and 1:6 ratios. Use the mix ratio specified by your engineer or project.

5. Does the total include scaffolding and transport?

No. The estimate includes cement, sand and entered wall and ceiling labour charges only. Scaffolding, transport, water, surface preparation and other additional costs are excluded.

6. Can I download the estimate?

Yes. Download CSV for spreadsheet use or select Print / Save PDF to save a printable report.
