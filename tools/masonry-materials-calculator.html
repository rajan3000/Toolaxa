📘 How to Use the Masonry Materials Calculator

1. Select the brick or block type

Choose red clay bricks, fly ash bricks or AAC blocks. Standard unit dimensions are automatically filled in and can be adjusted to match the actual product.

2. Enter wall dimensions

Enter the combined wall length, wall height, number of similar walls and wall thickness. Deduct the combined door and window opening area to calculate net wall area.

3. Enter mortar settings

For conventional masonry, select a cement-to-sand ratio such as 1:4 or 1:6. For AAC masonry, choose thin-bed adhesive and enter the manufacturer's recommended consumption.

4. Enter local material prices

Update the price per brick or block, cement bag, sand CFT and AAC adhesive kilogram according to your local supplier quotations.

5. Calculate and download

Click Calculate Masonry Materials to see estimated quantities and costs. Download CSV for spreadsheet use or print the estimate as a PDF.

How are bricks and mortar estimated?

The calculator estimates the net wall volume after deducting openings. It then estimates the number of masonry units using the entered unit dimensions and mortar joint allowance. The difference between wall volume and masonry unit volume is used as the preliminary wet mortar volume. A dry-volume factor is applied to estimate cement and sand quantities.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many bricks are required for a wall?

The quantity depends on wall area, wall thickness, actual brick dimensions and mortar joint thickness. Enter these details in the calculator to get an estimate.

Can I calculate fly ash bricks and AAC blocks?

Yes. Select the required material type and check the pre-filled dimensions. You can edit the dimensions to match your supplier's product.

Does the calculator deduct door and window openings?

Yes. Enter the combined area of all doors and windows. This area is deducted from the gross wall area before estimating masonry quantities.

Can I calculate cement and sand for brick mortar?

Yes. Select cement-sand mortar and choose the required ratio. The calculator estimates cement bags and sand quantity from the calculated mortar volume.

Can I estimate AAC block adhesive?

Yes. Select AAC Blocks and AAC Thin-Bed Adhesive. Enter the adhesive consumption specified by the manufacturer to estimate the required quantity.

Does this calculator include labour charges?

No. The estimate covers the entered masonry materials only. Labour, transportation, scaffolding, taxes, plaster and other construction costs are excluded.

Is this masonry calculator free?

Yes. You can use the Toolaxa Masonry Materials Calculator online without a calculation fee and download your estimate as a CSV or printable PDF.
